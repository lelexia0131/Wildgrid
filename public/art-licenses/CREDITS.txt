Wildgrid miniature camp art

Rock Moss Set 01 by Kless Gyzen, published by Poly Haven under CC0 1.0.
Six scans were simplified, scaled and colour graded for the playable rock terrain.
https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_moss_set_01
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Camp facilities, vegetation, landscape, icons and material processing are authored
for Wildgrid. Ground fibres and the source material sheet use project-specific AI-generated
imagery. The playable valley, river, sky and distant landscape are rendered geometry. The reference images supplied
by the user are internal design references.
